Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Usage Data is collected automatically during your use of the Service. This may include:

  • Device’s IP address

We may also collect mobile device identifiers and diagnostic information to improve performance and stability.

Our Applications may offer in-app purchases for additional content, features, or virtual items.
